# Research sources and model limits

The generator uses the FAO crop-factor table for Kc stage patterns: [FAO Crop water needs](https://www.fao.org/4/s2022e/s2022e07.htm). FAO's crop-evapotranspiration framework expresses crop demand as ETc = Kc × ETo: [FAO-56](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e00.htm). Pepper-specific water and scheduling context is documented by [FAO](https://www.fao.org/land-water/databases-and-software/crop-information/pepper/en/).

All generated records are research-informed synthetic simulations, not field observations. The resulting models must be calibrated with local ETo, crop cultivar, sensor calibration, soil analysis, irrigation-system efficiency, and measured outcomes before production use.
